Before you book

Terms & Conditions

Booking & payment

  • Deposit of RM300 shall be made in order to secure the date and the remaining payment shall be made at least 7 days before the event date or at the event date.
  • No refund for deposit due to reserve date and time.
  • Once the deposit has been paid, we consider as you have agreed all the terms and conditions.

Delivery

  • Preview (teaser) photos will be delivered up to 20-30 photos (highlights) within 1-3 days after the photoshoot.
  • All edited photos will be delivered within 1-3 weeks after the photoshoot by cloud drive.
  • Video will be shared within 4-8 weeks via cloud drive.
  • Album will be delivered within 1-2 months after draft layout proofing (may change depends on layout proofing by client).
  • Clients are responsible for downloading and backing up all photos within the 7‑day period from the date of delivery.

On the day

  • Additional cost of RM200 per hour will be charged in case client request to stay back after or if any photoshoot required after the coverage hour.
  • To ensure consistency & quality, our photographer will not take photos using personal mobile phones or devices.

Cancellation & liability

  • Last-minute cancellations are subject to a 50% cancellation fee.
  • In the event of cancellation by the photographer, or in the very unlikely case of total photographic failure, liability shall be limited to a full refund of any fees paid.

Rights

  • All assets produced remain the intellectual property of UNAX ARTS and may be used without further consent or compensation.
  • The UNAX ARTS reserves the right to the final decision in any situation of dispute.
  • Other terms & conditions may be appended or removed from time to time.
